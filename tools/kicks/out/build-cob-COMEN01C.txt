01061         *                      PROCESS-ENTER-KEY
01062         * The original right-justifies the typed option with reference
01063         * modification (OPTIONI(1:WS-IDX)); the field is 2 bytes so a
01064         * REDEFINES does the same job here.  This compiler has no STRING
01065         * or INSPECT verbs at all, so messages are assembled by MOVEing
01066         * into a group of fixed-width fields.

01071              IF WS-OPTION-X2 = SPACE OR WS-OPTION-X2 = LOW-VALUE
01072                  MOVE WS-OPTION-X1         TO WS-OPTION-X2
01073                  MOVE '0'                  TO WS-OPTION-X1.
01074              IF WS-OPTION-X1 = SPACE OR WS-OPTION-X1 = LOW-VALUE
01075                  MOVE '0'                  TO WS-OPTION-X1.
01076              IF WS-OPTION-X2 = SPACE OR WS-OPTION-X2 = LOW-VALUE
01077                  MOVE '0'                  TO WS-OPTION-X2.
01078              MOVE WS-OPTION-X              TO WS-OPTION.
01079              MOVE WS-OPTION                TO OPTIONO OF COMEN1AO.
01080  20

01085                  MOVE 'Please enter a valid option number...' TO
01086                                  WS-MESSAGE
01087                  PERFORM SEND-MENU-SCREEN.

01089              IF ERR-FLG-OFF AND CDEMO-USRTYP-USER AND
01090                 CDEMO-MENU-OPT-USRTYPE(WS-OPTION) = 'A'
01091                  MOVE 'Y'                TO WS-ERR-FLG
